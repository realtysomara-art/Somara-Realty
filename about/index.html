About Somara

“Some people are born into opportunity. I had to build mine.”

Somara Realty exists because its founder, Amit Thakur, turned repeated setbacks — in business, in sales, in life — into a method rather than a memory. No capital, no safety net, no clear path. Just the decision, made over and over, to keep showing up. The brand carries that same posture into every client relationship: substance over flash, and trust earned rather than assumed.
